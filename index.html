Windowsill math

The herb garden that outgrew the sill, the row of terracotta that is heavier than it looks, and the watering can that never holds enough for the week. Windowsill math counts the pots that actually fit, the kilograms of wet soil they add up to, and the liters you will carry every week. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Pot plan - sill and pot become across, rows and a pot count.
  • Weight plan - pots and soil become liters and kilograms on the sill.
  • Water plan - pots and an interval become liters to carry each week.

Labeled norms

Labeled pot: 14 cm across. Labeled wet soil: 1.3 kg a liter. Labeled drink: 250 ml per pot every 3 days. These are illustrative estimates, not structural advice - your sill, your pots and your plants decide the real numbers.
